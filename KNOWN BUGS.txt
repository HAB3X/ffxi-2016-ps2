FFXI 2016 PS2 Edition: known bugs
Fan Project by Habex
==================================

GAME
  1. Burning Circles can't be targeted, so orb battles can't be entered.
  2. Floor textures flash and pop in and out in certain areas.
  3. Scrolling quickly through long lists causes large dips in framerate.
  4. If signing in can't reach the server, the game doesn't go back to the sign in page
     so you can fix the details. You have to restart the disc.
