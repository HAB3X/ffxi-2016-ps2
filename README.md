# FFXI 2016 PS2 Edition

Fan Project by Habex

Play the 2016 PS2 version of Final Fantasy XI on your own server, in PCSX2 or on a real PS2 with a hard drive.

## Get it

From the **Releases** page on the right, download all three files:

1. `FFXI-2016.zip`
2. `FFXI.2016.Patch.ffxipatch.001`
3. `FFXI.2016.Patch.ffxipatch.002`

Unzip the first one. Put both patch parts in the `Disc/Patch` folder and your original disc image in `Disc/Original Disc`. Then open `READ ME.txt` and follow the steps.

## Found a bug?

**[Report it here](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**. You can add screenshots.

Known bugs:

1. Burning Circles can't be targeted, so orb battles can't be entered.
2. Floor textures flash and pop in and out in certain areas.
3. Scrolling quickly through long lists causes large dips in framerate.
4. If signing in can't reach the server, the game doesn't go back to the sign in page. You have to restart the disc.
